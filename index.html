About the Atlas & its method

What this map is

This map shows the publicly documented relationships and structural roles connecting Singapore’s space institutions, research community, companies, programmes and supporting ecosystem — not an exhaustive representation of every company or every relationship.

What is shown

The map draws the featured anchors; the full catalogued set (featured + others) lives in the catalogue and search. Exact counts are shown in the catalogue and are derived from the data; only the “~70 companies” denominator is a cited editorial estimate (OSTIn, 2025/26 Space Industry Directory).

How relationships are evidenced

Every relationship carries its own sources and two independent labels: confidence — documented (explicit public evidence) vs inferred (reasonable interpretation, hidden by default); and pathway — direct, programme-mediated or contextual. Line style encodes pathway; line opacity encodes confidence. Shapes encode entity type; colour encodes stakeholder type; position encodes structural layer.

What is not claimed

  • No exhaustive inventory of companies or relationships.
  • Absence of an edge is not a claim that no relationship exists.
  • A visible node does not imply a direct NSAS relationship.
